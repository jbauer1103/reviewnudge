ReviewWhistle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

ReviewWhistle is operated by Bauer Eco Management Solution Ltd. of North Vancouver, British Columbia ("we", "us"). It helps local service businesses ask their customers for Google reviews. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. It covers our website (reviewwhistle.com), our web app (app.reviewwhistle.com), our mobile apps, and our customer review pages.

Who this applies to

  • Business owners who sign up for ReviewWhistle ("owners").
  • Customers of those businesses, whose details an owner adds so they can ask them for a review.

For customer information, the owner decides what to add and who to contact. We store and process it on the owner's behalf.

What we collect

From owners

  • Your email address, used to log you in.
  • Your business name, Google listing, review link, public Google review count and the review message you write.
  • Billing details are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number; we keep only your Stripe customer ID and subscription status.

About customers (added by owners)

  • Name, phone number and, optionally, email address.
  • A record of when the owner asked them for a review.
  • Any private feedback a customer chooses to send through a ReviewWhistle review page.

Phone contacts

If an owner taps "Pick from phone contacts", the app reads only the one contact they choose, to fill in the name and phone number. We do not upload or store the rest of your address book.

Text messages

ReviewWhistle does not send text messages. When an owner asks for a review, the app opens the phone's own Messages app with a pre-written text, and the owner chooses whether to send it. We do not read your messages.

How we use information

  • To run the service: log you in, store your customer list, open the review text, show your dashboard.
  • To send one follow-up email to a customer 48 hours after a review request, if the owner added their email and they haven't been emailed about it before.
  • To show your Google review count, which we look up from Google's public listing.
  • To bill your subscription and send account emails (login links, receipts, billing notices).
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use customer information for our own marketing.

Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run ReviewWhistle. They only process information to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, login and server functions (data stored in Canada, ca-central-1).
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
  • Resend: sending emails.
  • Google: looking up business listings and public review counts.
  • Expo and Netlify: hosting the web app and website.

Some of these providers process information in the United States. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Owners' responsibilities

Owners must only add customers they have a business relationship with and are allowed to contact, following the laws that apply to them, including Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) and the U.S. CAN-SPAM Act and TCPA.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When an owner deletes a customer, that customer's details are deleted. When an owner deletes their account (Settings → Delete account), we delete their login, business, customers, review requests and feedback, and cancel any Stripe subscription. Stripe keeps payment records as required by law.

Your choices and rights

  • Owners can view, edit or delete their information in the app at any time.
  • Customers who want their details removed, or who have a question about information a business added, can contact that business or email us and we will help.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under Canada's PIPEDA or U.S. state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information. Email us to make a request.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Each business can only ever see its own data, enforced in the database itself. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects you, as required by law.

Children

ReviewWhistle is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we make important changes to this policy, we will update the date above and let owners know by email or in the app.
