## Full description
ReviewWhistle helps local service businesses get more Google reviews.

After a job, pick the customer and tap one button. Your phone's own Messages app opens with a friendly, ready-to-send text that includes your Google review link. You hit send, so it comes from your number and feels personal.

- Add customers by hand or from your phone contacts
- Edit your review message to sound like you
- One reminder email 48 hours later, if the customer gave you their email
- Customers who had a problem can tell you privately instead of posting it
- A dashboard of requests sent and Google reviews gained

Made for cleaners, plumbers, HVAC, auto detailers, dog groomers and other local businesses in Canada and the US.

An account is required. New businesses get a 14-day free trial.

## App Review notes (Apple)
ReviewWhistle is a free companion app for a business service that owners subscribe to on our website (guideline 3.1.3(f)). The app has no purchasing and no links to buy. To log in: type the demo email below on the first screen. A password box appears (only for this email); enter the password and tap "Log in". The account has a sample business, customers and history.
