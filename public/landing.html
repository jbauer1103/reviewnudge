Questions

Good questions owners ask.

Does ReviewNudge send texts for me?

No. It opens your phone's own Messages app with the text ready to go, and you press send. That way the text comes from your number and you stay in control of every message.

Is this allowed by Google?

Yes. Google wants businesses to ask for reviews. ReviewNudge shows every customer the Google review button and never hides it from anyone, and it never offers rewards for reviews.

What if a customer doesn't have an email?

That's fine. The text is the main ask. The 48-hour reminder email only goes out when you've saved an email for that customer.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. ReviewNudge uses the regular Messages app on both.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can delete your account from Settings at any time, and your customer list and history are deleted with it.
